# Optional independent scientific critique

An external review service is **not required** to derive, implement or run this diagnostic. No account access, paid computation, GPU, laboratory work or user action is needed for this stage. Another model's agreement would not establish novelty or biological validity. We have not contacted any external service or uploaded this private project.

If the user wants an independent review, paste this prompt with `LEARNING_RULE.md`, `MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT.md`, `PROTOCOL.md` and the result report. Share only the material the user chooses to disclose.

> Act as a critical reviewer of an early nature-inspired learning proposal. Do not improve its marketing or assume novelty. Check the exact change of variables from two assembly concentrations to a coefficient and total occupancy. Identify any algebraic errors, missing capacity costs, nonlocal operations or biological assumptions unsupported by the cited primary work. Distinguish exact algorithmic equivalence from overlap in representation or analogy. Find closer primary precedents for the finite-pool update and error-correlated feature-admission rule, giving precise links and access limits. Assess whether the synthetic controls and resource comparisons support the reported claims. Suggest the smallest experiment that could falsify the remaining hypothesis. State what the present model cannot do. If AM-0 is only a constrained polynomial learner, say so. For a future temporal-assembly-pathway learner, identify the specific operation that would have to change; do not simply propose another neural network with biological names. Do not produce wet-lab protocols or biological sequences.

A biophysicist or chemical-computing researcher becomes valuable if a future rule survives this mathematical gate, particularly to assess physically local credit assignment and selective transfer. A useful review must engage with equations and counterexamples; consensus among language models is not the success metric.
